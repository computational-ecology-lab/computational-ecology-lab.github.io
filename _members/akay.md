description: "I am a PhD student in the Computational Ecology Lab, co-supervised by Dr Miguel Lurgi and Dr Nicole Esteban, and working in collaboration with researchers from the Institute of Zoology at ZSL London. My PhD aims to understand the biodiversity and ecological value of barachois, a type of coastal lagoon, across the Indian Ocean.


My research focuses on understanding the ecological communities found within coral reefs and their adjacent habitats, how populations interact with one another, and how these communities may be influenced by climate change and anthropogenic disturbances.


I completed my BSc and MSc in Marine Biology at the University of Portsmouth. During my MSc, my thesis investigated how structural complexity in coral reefs could be estimated using a morphology-based index. Following my studies, I spent a few years working on biomedical research projects at the University of Oxford.


Outside of research, I enjoy cooking, going to the gym, and gaming."
