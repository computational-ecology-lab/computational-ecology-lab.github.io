Last Saturday 24th August 2026, I had the pleasure of talking to Bridgend locals about microalgae in the science tent at [**Broadland Fun Day**](https://broadlandsfunday.co.uk/). The science tent included a variety of science themed stalls from the physics of every day life to antibiotic resistance. The activities I organised for my table included crafting with paint made from spirulina, microscopy and using a vortex to re-suspend cultures. I shared my knowledge with children and parents about the world of microorganisms and how microalgae specifically play a role in the world around us. 

**Special thanks to Alex Caswell and Timothy Ogunrin for helping out at my stall and to Dr Geertje van Keulen for organising the Science tent.**
